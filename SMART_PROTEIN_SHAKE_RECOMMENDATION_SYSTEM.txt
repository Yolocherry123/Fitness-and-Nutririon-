SMART PROTEIN & SHAKE RECOMMENDATION SYSTEM
SPECIFICATION FOR PERSONAL FITNESS APP

PURPOSE
====================================================

The app must determine whether a whey/protein shake is actually useful based on foods checked off as consumed during the day.

The shake is NOT a compulsory daily item.

CORE PRINCIPLE:

The app estimates:
1. Protein already consumed
2. Protein likely from remaining planned meals
3. Expected total protein for the day
4. Estimated protein gap

Then it decides whether:
- No shake is needed
- A shake is optional
- Additional food protein should be prioritized
- A whey shake is a convenient recommendation

The app should answer:

"Based on what I have eaten and what I still plan to eat, do I need a shake today?"

====================================================
USER PROTEIN TARGET
====================================================

Primary daily protein target: 115 g

Preferred target range: 110–125 g protein/day

These values must be editable in Settings and must not be permanently hard-coded.

====================================================
IMPORTANT: ESTIMATES ARE NOT EXACT
====================================================

Hostel food portions and recipes vary.

The app must label calculations as:
"Estimated protein"

It must support:

1. EXACT LOGGING
   When nutrition information and quantity are known.

2. APPROXIMATE LOGGING
   When the user selects food and a rough portion.

3. CHECKLIST-ONLY LOGGING
   When a meal is simply marked consumed without reliable macro data.

Never present unknown hostel meal nutrition as exact.

====================================================
FOOD CHECK-OFF SYSTEM
====================================================

When a food is checked off, add estimated protein when sufficient information exists.

Example: Anabolic Bowl

Calculate from the actual recipe/configuration selected.

Typical components may include:
- oats
- milk OR prepared milk-powder drink
- peanut butter
- optional additions

Milk powder should preferably use the nutrition information from the user's specific product label because brands vary.

====================================================
HOSTEL BREAKFAST LOGGING
====================================================

Do not rely only on a generic checkbox called "Hostel Breakfast".

Allow the user to:

1. Mark breakfast consumed
2. Select major protein sources actually eaten

Example:

[x] Hostel breakfast
[x] 2 eggs
[x] Milk

The app estimates protein from known protein sources.

====================================================
CURRENT VS EXPECTED PROTEIN
====================================================

Calculate:

1. CURRENT PROTEIN

CURRENT PROTEIN =
Sum of estimated protein from foods confirmed as CONSUMED

2. EXPECTED REMAINING PROTEIN

Protein likely from remaining scheduled foods with reasonable confidence.

Examples:
- remaining meals
- scheduled chicken/kebab
- known planned protein foods

Expected food must NOT be presented as already consumed.

3. EXPECTED DAILY PROTEIN

EXPECTED DAILY PROTEIN =
CURRENT PROTEIN + EXPECTED REMAINING PROTEIN

4. PROTEIN GAP

PROTEIN GAP =
PRIMARY PROTEIN TARGET - EXPECTED DAILY PROTEIN

====================================================
TIME-AWARE SHAKE LOGIC
====================================================

Recommendations must depend on time of day.

MORNING:
Do not recommend whey merely because current protein is low.

Example:
"It's early in the day. Focus on your planned meals first."

AFTERNOON:
Estimate likely daily total.

Example:
"Based on what you have eaten and today's remaining meals, prioritize protein later today."

EVENING:
A stronger recommendation is appropriate when few meal opportunities remain.

Example:
"You are estimated to finish below your protein target. A convenient protein addition may be useful tonight."

====================================================
SMART SHAKE DECISION ENGINE
====================================================

Do NOT use:

"Workout today = automatically drink a shake."

Evaluate:

A. Current estimated protein
B. Remaining planned meals
C. Scheduled protein additions
D. Expected daily protein
E. Current time
F. Protein target
G. Convenience and user preference

====================================================
RECOMMENDATION LEVELS
====================================================

GREEN — ON TRACK

Expected daily protein: 110–125 g or above minimum target.

Message:
"You're likely on track to meet your protein goal. A whey shake is probably unnecessary for protein today."

A shake may still be used for calories or convenience.

----------------------------------------------------

YELLOW — CLOSE

Expected daily protein: ~95–110 g

Message:
"You're reasonably close to your protein target. Check your remaining meals before deciding whether to add whey."

Actions:
- Add food protein
- Add whey
- Wait and reassess

----------------------------------------------------

ORANGE — LIKELY SHORT

Expected daily protein: ~80–95 g

Message:
"You're likely to finish below your protein target. Prioritize an additional protein source."

Options:
- Whey
- Chicken
- Eggs
- Paneer
- Curd/Yogurt
- Milk or milk-powder drink when useful

----------------------------------------------------

RED — SIGNIFICANTLY SHORT LATE IN THE DAY

Expected daily protein: below ~80 g AND few meals remain.

Message:
"Your protein intake appears significantly below your target today. Prioritize a convenient protein source."

Do NOT encourage extreme compensatory eating or multiple shakes automatically.

====================================================
FOOD-FIRST LOGIC
====================================================

When normal meals remain, prioritize food awareness before automatically recommending whey.

Priority:

1. Check remaining planned meals.
2. Check scheduled chicken/kebab.
3. Check available food protein.
4. Recommend whey if food is insufficient or inconvenient.

Example:

Current protein: 85 g
Remaining target: ~30 g
Dinner remains.

Recommendation:
"Check the protein available at dinner before adding a shake."

====================================================
EXAMPLE SCENARIO 1 — SHAKE NOT NEEDED
====================================================

Current consumed protein: 90 g
Remaining scheduled chicken: ~30 g
Expected dinner protein: ~10 g

Expected total: ~130 g

Recommendation:
GREEN

"You're likely to reach your protein target today. A shake is unnecessary for protein purposes."

====================================================
EXAMPLE SCENARIO 2 — SHAKE USEFUL
====================================================

Current consumed protein: 75 g
Expected remaining dinner protein: ~15 g

Expected total: ~90 g
Protein gap: ~25 g

Recommendation:
ORANGE

"You're likely to finish below your protein target. A convenient protein addition would be useful."

Actions:
[Add Whey Shake]
[Add Food Protein]

====================================================
EXAMPLE SCENARIO 3 — WAIT FOR DINNER
====================================================

Current consumed protein: 85 g
Dinner remains.
Dinner protein is unknown.

Recommendation:
YELLOW

"You're currently below your target, but dinner is still remaining. Check what protein is available before deciding on whey."

====================================================
EXAMPLE SCENARIO 4 — MORNING
====================================================

Current protein: 20 g
Time: 9:00 AM

Do NOT say:
"You're short by 95 g. Drink multiple shakes."

Instead:
"It's early in the day. Continue with your planned meals and track the main protein sources."

====================================================
SHAKE AS A CALORIE TOOL
====================================================

Protein and calorie recommendations must remain separate.

The app may conclude:

"Protein target is likely met."

while also saying:

"Additional calories may still be useful."

A shake can be used as:
1. Protein tool
2. Calorie tool
3. Convenience tool

The UI must explain WHY it is suggested.

Examples:
"Suggested because protein is likely low."

or

"Protein is sufficient, but this shake could help increase calories."

====================================================
CORE PSEUDOCODE
====================================================

CURRENT_PROTEIN =
sum(protein from foods marked CONSUMED)

EXPECTED_REMAINING_PROTEIN =
sum(protein from remaining scheduled foods with reasonable confidence)

EXPECTED_DAILY_PROTEIN =
CURRENT_PROTEIN + EXPECTED_REMAINING_PROTEIN

PROTEIN_GAP =
PROTEIN_TARGET - EXPECTED_DAILY_PROTEIN

IF early in day:
    Focus on planned meals first.

ELSE IF EXPECTED_DAILY_PROTEIN >= minimum_target:
    ON_TRACK
    Shake not needed for protein.

ELSE IF PROTEIN_GAP <= ~10 g:
    CLOSE
    Shake optional.

ELSE:
    ADD_PROTEIN_SOURCE

    If normal food is available:
        show food options.

    If food is unavailable or inconvenient:
        recommend whey as a convenient option.

====================================================
IMPORTANT SAFEGUARDS
====================================================

1. Never recommend whey automatically just because it is a workout day.

2. Never recommend multiple shakes merely because current protein is low early in the day.

3. Do not treat expected protein as consumed protein.

4. Clearly distinguish:
   - CONSUMED
   - EXPECTED
   - TARGET

5. Hostel estimates must be labeled approximate.

6. Allow the user to override portions.

7. Allow nutrition values to be edited.

8. Use actual product labels for whey and milk powder when configured.

9. Do not punish skipped whey in completion scoring.

10. Whey remains OPTIONAL unless the user customizes the plan.

FINAL PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
====================================================

The app should NOT ask:

"Did you drink your shake today?"

"Based on what you have consumed and what you are still likely to eat, is a shake useful today?"

The purpose is to make whey a smart nutrition tool rather than a compulsory daily checkbox.
